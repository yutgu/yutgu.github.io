Teaching

Whether a student goes on to graduate school, clinical practice, or a career outside psychology, I want them to leave my courses able to evaluate a claim about human behavior, recognize the evidence behind it, and identify when that evidence falls short. I have organized my teaching around three practices: making the implicit norms of the discipline explicit, building complex skills through structured practice, and connecting scientific thinking to students’ own lives and goals.
